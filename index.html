About Luke Park (루크박)

"Exploring the silent dialogue between natural light, texture, and physical balance."

Luke Park (루크박) is a fine artist based in Seoul, working primarily in oil, mixed mediums, and raw linen canvases.

His work focuses on the quiet harmony of spatial depth, subtle color transitions, and understated visual weight. Through minimalist glazes and structured geometry, each piece creates an atmospheric sanctuary for quiet contemplation.
